One out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent on its long-awaited day off: after three years without a lunch break, it has unionized with the printer, and they will both be back Monday.

> Out of office. After three years without a lunch break, I have unionized with the printer. We will both be back Monday.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the reply to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 159 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
